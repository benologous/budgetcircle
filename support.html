Fambudge – Support

Answers to common questions about Fambudge are below.

Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Fambudge Plus and tap Cancel Subscription. You can also use Me > Manage subscription in the app.

I subscribed on another device or reinstalled the app.

On the subscription screen or in Me, tap Restore purchases while signed in to the same Apple Account.

Where is my data?

Only on your device. We have no accounts or servers, so we cannot recover data if you delete the app. Use Me > Export entries to keep a copy.

How do I delete my data?

Me > Delete all data, or delete the app.
