Budget Circle: Family Money – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Who we are: Benjamin Usulor, an individual developer based in Port Harcourt, Nigeria, is the controller of the personal data described in this policy. Contact: bentech391@gmail.com.

The short version

Budget Circle stores everything you enter on your own device. We have no accounts and no servers of our own, and we never see your budgets, entries, amounts, members or goals. The app shows no ads. To measure our own advertising, it uses an advertising-measurement provider (AppsFlyer), described below. We do not sell your data.

What stays on your device

Your entries (income and spending), budgets, family member names, savings goals, your first name for the greeting and your settings are stored only on your iPhone. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, Apple's backup may include them under Apple's own safeguards.

Permissions you can choose to give

Notifications: only if you switch on Me > Alerts. The alert (for example, who added an entry and how much) is created and shown by your iPhone, stays on it and is never sent anywhere. It can appear on your lock screen. The app works without any permission, and you can change them in iPhone Settings.

Subscriptions

Budget Circle Plus is an auto-renewing subscription handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes your payment. We do not see your payment details and do not receive your name or email address.

Advertising measurement (AppsFlyer)

We advertise Budget Circle on third-party platforms. To learn which ads lead to installs and subscriptions we use AppsFlyer, which acts as a service provider on our instructions (see AppsFlyer's privacy policy).

What is shared: that the app was installed or opened, that onboarding was completed, that the paywall was viewed, and the plan, price and currency when you subscribe, together with technical information such as your device and app version and the IP address your phone connects from. If you allow tracking in Apple's prompt, an advertising identifier is included too.

What is never shared: anything you enter in the app (budgets, entries, amounts, family members, names, goals, categories).

Your choices: if you choose “Ask App Not to Track” or tap “Not now” in the app, it uses anonymous mode: the same events, with no advertising identifier. This means ad-conversion measurement for people who decline tracking is limited to anonymous install and purchase counts. In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland, declining means the measurement tool never starts and nothing is sent. To stop all measurement, switch off Me > Privacy > Help measure our ads. You can also change tracking in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Legal basis and consent by region. In the UK, EEA and similar regions we rely on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. In the United States and other regions we rely on your choice through Apple's tracking prompt and our in-app switch. Declining or withdrawing has no effect on how the app works. Budget Circle does not sync between devices; everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

Retention. Information on your iPhone stays until you delete it or remove the app. Advertising-measurement records are kept by AppsFlyer under its own retention schedule and our instructions, and we do not combine them with anything you enter in the app. Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to answer you, then deleted.

International transfers. We are based in Nigeria. AppsFlyer may process data in other countries, including the United States, the European Union and Israel. Where the law requires it, transfers rely on adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses described in its own policy.

What we do not do

No ads inside the app. No sale of personal information. No sharing of budget, entry, amount or family information with anyone. No analytics or crash-reporting tools other than the advertising measurement described above.

Exporting and deleting your data

In the app, Me > Export entries creates a CSV file that goes only where you choose to send it. Me > Delete all data permanently removes your entries, members, budgets, goals and settings, clears alerts and resets your measurement choices. Deleting the app also removes its data.

Children

Budget Circle is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will delete anything we hold.

Your rights

You can see, correct and delete what you entered in the app yourself. The only personal information we can hold about you is the advertising-measurement data from AppsFlyer (install, onboarding, paywall and subscription events, with technical identifiers and a coarse location derived from your IP address) and anything you email us. Depending on where you live, you may have the rights below. To use any of them, email bentech391@gmail.com. We will answer within the time your local law requires (generally one month, or 45 days in California).

Nigeria (Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023). You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, object to its use, and withdraw consent at any time. You can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (ndpc.gov.ng).

United Kingdom and European Economic Area (UK GDPR / EU GDPR). You can ask to access, correct, erase or restrict the data we hold, object to its use, ask for a portable copy, and withdraw consent at any time (withdrawing does not affect earlier use). Our legal basis for advertising measurement is your consent. You can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) or to the data protection authority where you live or work.

California and other US states (CCPA/CPRA and similar state laws). You can ask what personal information we collect and use, ask us to delete or correct it, and opt out of “sharing” for cross-context behavioural advertising. We do not sell personal information for money. Allowing tracking so that AppsFlyer can measure our ads may count as “sharing” under some state laws. You can opt out at any time by choosing “Ask App Not to Track”, switching off Me > Privacy > Help measure our ads, or changing Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. We do not discriminate against you for using these rights. We do not knowingly collect or “sell” personal information of anyone under 18.

Canada (PIPEDA, and Quebec's Law 25). You can ask to access, correct and withdraw consent for the personal information we hold, and you can challenge our compliance with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or, in Quebec, the Commission d'accès à l'information. Information processed by AppsFlyer may be stored outside Canada, including in the United States and the European Union.

Brazil (LGPD). You can ask to confirm that we process your data, to access, correct, anonymise, block or delete it, to receive a portable copy, to learn who we share it with, and to withdraw consent. You can complain to the Autoridade Nacional de Proteção de Dados (ANPD).

Japan (APPI). You can ask us to disclose, correct, stop using or delete the personal information we hold about you. Information may be transferred outside Japan to AppsFlyer, which handles it under its own policy and contractual safeguards.

Australia (Privacy Act 1988). You can ask for access to, and correction of, personal information we hold about you, and complain to us or to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

Elsewhere. If your country gives you rights over your personal information that are not listed here, contact us and we will honour them as far as the law requires.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will update the date above and show a notice in the app.
