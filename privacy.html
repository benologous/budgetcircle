Budget Circle: Family Money – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

The short version

Budget Circle stores everything you enter on your own device. We have no accounts and no servers of our own, and we never see your budgets, entries, amounts, members or goals. To measure our own advertising, the app uses an advertising-measurement provider (AppsFlyer), described below. We do not sell your data.

What stays on your device

Your entries (income and spending), budgets, family member names and savings goals are stored only on your iPhone. They are not sent to us or to anyone else.

Subscriptions

Budget Circle Plus is an auto-renewing subscription handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes your payment. We do not see your payment details and do not receive your name or email address.

Advertising measurement (AppsFlyer)

So we can tell which of our ads led to an installation or subscription, the app uses AppsFlyer. With your permission through iPhone's tracking request, AppsFlyer receives your device's advertising identifier, whether the app was installed or opened, and when you subscribe (the plan, price and currency). If you decline, it receives only anonymous install information with no device identifiers. Your budgets, entries, amounts, family members, goals and categories are never shared. You can turn this off at any time in the app under Me > Privacy, or in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. AppsFlyer's own policy is at appsflyer.com/legal/privacy-policy.

Exporting and deleting your data

In the app, Me > Export entries creates a CSV file that goes only where you choose to send it. Me > Delete all data removes everything from your device. Deleting the app also removes its data.

Children

Budget Circle is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes
